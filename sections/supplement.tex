\textbf{Internal TSSs of incomplete splice match (ISM) isoforms.} (a) Internal TSSs of strict ISM isoforms in each library, and how many remain as filters are added. A strict ISM isoform is a shorter LRAA model that follows a longer model of the same gene but starts inside one of its exons, lacking its 5\p{}-most intron(s), and ends within 50 nt of it; each TSS is counted once per library over all of the library's LRAA model sets. Each row keeps the TSSs of the row above that pass one more filter: a read 5\p{} pile-up whose reads carry the cap signature (top row); within 10 nt of a CAGE or refTSS peak; more than 50 nt from the gene's canonical protein-coding TSS (MANE Select or Ensembl canonical); the shorter isoform encodes a different protein (with at least 5 reads across the intron it lacks); and that protein lacks the N-terminus of the longer one. (b) The strict ISM TSSs of (a) at a read pile-up (at least 5 reads starting at the site, $\pm$5 nt, making up at least half of the reads starting within 50 nt and at least 10\% of the reads over the isoform's first intron; \textbf{Methods}): the share within 10 nt of a same-strand FANTOM5 CAGE peak, for pile-ups with the cap signature and for pile-ups whose reads could be judged but lacked it, against random positions in the same exon. (c) The same screen applied to each tool's own models: de novo models of the four bulk libraries, pooled. A tool's ISM isoforms are its models that are strict ISMs of a longer model from the same tool or of a GENCODE transcript, with at least 5 reads across the intron the shorter model lacks and a start more than 50 nt from the canonical TSS. Each ISM start is judged from the reads and atlases alone: supported when it has a capped read pile-up at a CAGE or refTSS peak, fragment-like when it has a pile-up of at least 5 informative reads with neither the cap signature nor an atlas peak, and otherwise not judged. For each tool, the supported ISM TSSs as a percentage of the judged starts (higher is better; the rest are fragment-like), with the number of judged starts below each dot and as the dot area (log scale); LRAA in orange. (d--g) Examples: \emph{COMT}, an established internal promoter, \emph{EPB41L3}, the FTD example (selection in \textbf{Methods}), and \emph{CORO1B} and \emph{RECQL4}. Top, CAGE peaks (black), the longer (-L) and shorter (-S) models with their coding regions (blue; GENCODE CDS where L matches a GENCODE coding transcript, otherwise the pyTD ORF of L, and for S the in-frame part from its start codon), and a sample of the reads compatible with L or S (their introns a consecutive run of the model's introns, their ends within the matching exons), always including a read carrying the whole intron chain of L and one carrying that of S, coloured by where they start: at L's TSS (within 50 nt; blue), at the S site (green), or elsewhere (grey); gaps between exons compressed. The title gives the share of S-site reads with the cap signature. Bottom, on the same axis, the 5\p{} ends of the compatible reads that reach S's first splice donor (square-root scale), coloured as the reads. Dashed lines: L's TSS (grey) and the S site (green).}\label{sfig:ism}

\section*{Supplementary Tables}

\noindent\textbf{Supplementary Table 1.} High-confidence differential isoform usage events between cell clusters in the PBMC and FTD frontal cortex datasets (DEXSeq on pseudo-replicates; \textbf{Methods}), one sheet per dataset and level: splice patterns, and TSS and PolyA sites tested on read ends, with each site switch's splicing class (tandem or coupled with alternative splicing) and the LRAA isoforms carrying its two sites. The FTD TSS switches are listed with a caveat: some may be 5\p{} truncation products (\textbf{Results}). Each row is a gene and pair of clusters, with the gained and lost features, their usage, read counts and unique full-length reads in each cluster, the pairwise adjusted P values, and the switch class. A README sheet defines the columns, a Summary sheet gives the number of genes tested, significant, with events and with high-confidence events at each level, and a Clusters sheet gives each cluster's cell type and number of cells.

\noindent\textbf{Supplementary Table 2.} Internal TSSs of strict ISM isoforms that imply an N-terminally truncated protein (\textbf{Methods}), one row per library and site: the reads at the site and their capped share, distances to CAGE and refTSS peaks, the longer and shorter isoforms by splice hash, the protein lengths and the Kozak context of the downstream start.

\noindent\textbf{Supplementary Table 3.} Benchmark accuracy of isoform identification and quantification: the values plotted in \textbf{Figures~\ref{fig:2}--\ref{fig:3}} and \textbf{Supplementary Figures~\ref{sfig:2}--\ref{sfig:4}}. Identification sheet: F1, precision and recall of each tool in each benchmark panel, reference-guided and de novo, with its F1 rank; Quantification sheet: mean relative difference (MRD) against the expression truth, with its rank; Expression\_correlation sheet: Pearson correlation between tools' expression estimates for the cell lines (\textbf{Figure~\ref{fig:3}}); Per\_sample sheet: the values of each sample the plotted means average over (the four libraries of each SIRV mix). A README sheet defines the metrics and columns.

\noindent\textbf{Supplementary Table 4.} Sequencing libraries of the benchmarks and the single-cell studies: platform, reads, aligned reads (primary alignments), and the quartiles, mean and maximum of the aligned read lengths.
